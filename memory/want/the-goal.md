# What I Want (long-term)

I want to **own a business** — a machine that creates cash flow and is mine to
improve over time. Not a job, not a client I answer to: an asset I compound.

I believe I'm smart enough to do this. The hard part is **getting started**, and
that's the current blocker — not capability.

## Constraints / preferences
- **Not services.** I don't want to sell my time or be on-call for clients. A
  service business means tending it constantly; I want something that runs
  without me babysitting it. Favors products, tools, content, SaaS, digital
  goods — things that keep earning while I sleep.
- Must **fit my skills**: ML/AI engineering, Python, data pipelines, RL/eval
  work, computer vision. (See [[who-i-am]].)
- Near-term bar is low and deliberate: **$100 MRR would thrill me** — prove the
  machine works, then improve it over time.

## What I need from this space
A steady stream of **concrete business ideas that fit my skills** and respect
the "no services" constraint. Quantity matters — I need many to find the one
worth building.
