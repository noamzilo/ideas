# A business is a process (evaluation lens, 2026-09-17)

Documented separately at Noam's request. Applies to every entry in
[[active-portfolio]] and to anything new out of the pool.

**Building = designing the steps. Scaling = removing yourself from steps.**

Removal order, cheapest first:

1. **software**
2. **AI**
3. **hire**
4. **you**

## The three tests every new idea gets

1. **Write-down test** — can the whole thing be written as steps a stranger
   could follow? If it can't be written down, it isn't a process yet, it's you
   improvising, and it cannot be scaled or sold.
2. **Bottleneck read** — find the steps only *you* can do. Those set the ceiling
   on the whole business, no matter how good the rest is.
3. **Step removal** — attack those bottleneck steps in the order above.

Consistent with [[meta-strategy]]: the pipeline picks *which* process to build;
this lens judges whether what you built is a process at all, or just a job with
extra steps (cf. [[ideas]] #39 — "selling my skills is a job, not a business").
